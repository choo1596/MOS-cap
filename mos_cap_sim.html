Reset device returns a fresh device that was never connected (slide 8): no charge, flat bands, EFm − EF,Si = −eφms.
Opening and closing the switch does not flatten the bands: opening traps the charge, and closing again drives the device toward the source VG. To get flat bands with the wire connected, close the switch and press the VFB preset (slide 10).
